About Me

Hello! I'm Keya and I made this website to share my art and creative projects. I enjoy making pixel art, digital illustrations, and experimenting with different styles just for fun.

Most of my art is inspired by:

  • Retro pixel aesthetics
  • Cyberpunk colors and neon glow
  • Video game art styles
  • Random creative ideas that pop into my head

This website is my little portfolio where I can collect my drawings and show them to people. I'm still learning web design and coding, but building this site has been really fun.

Thanks for visiting my art page! ✨
